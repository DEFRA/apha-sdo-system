## Applied with an interpretation

- **The 30-day sentence is not repeated under the date field.** The document has "Please try to submit …" there as well as "You must submit …" above it.
- **"For example, 08 2026"**, not "08 26": the year box takes four digits.
- **The "Month covered" label stays visible** on all three date pages. The document shows "Month and year of sample" for Bat rabies and no label for AHR, but comment 106 asks for validation errors to say "Month covered", and the engine words errors from the label.
- **Confirmation statement** uses "each test’s associated UKAS accreditation status" (sheet, agreed 2 November) rather than "each one's" (document, 26 October).
- **"UKAS accreditation"** is lower case throughout, including the Change link (the document kept "Accreditation").
- **Web form check your answers** says "Month your report covers" rather than "Report date", to match the upload journeys and answer Liz's comment that it reads like the submission date.
- **File name hint** uses the sheet's agreed "… that your report covers …", not the document's "… that you collected your data …" (marked "Received? CHECK"). The example uses the report's own month, e.g. "Report-2024-03.xlsx" for March 2024.
- **"Report submitted"** also shows on the developer example form, which uses the same engine page.
